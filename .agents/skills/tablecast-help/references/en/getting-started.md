## Choose a store

[Open TableCast](/admin/live) and sign in when prompted. Check the organisations and stores available to your account, then select the store at the top of the sidebar. Accept staff invitations using the invited account.

## Prepare the menu and service

Open products, categories, plans and service settings under Menu & service. Provide Japanese and English names, confirmed prices, required choices and availability. Save changes as a draft, then follow [Configuration and publishing](./configuration.md) to review and apply them.

You can ask an agent to prepare a bilingual draft from menu photographs, leaving unreadable prices pending. Start with [MCP connections](./mcp.md).

## Prepare the tabletop iPad

Register the device from the store’s device page and confirm its store and table. Allow microphone access on the iPad. Check the menu, required choices, basket and order confirmation. See [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md) if voice is unavailable.

## During service

Use the floor view to check visits and orders. Confirming an order requires a review of its prices and choices. Stopping voice preserves the basket and checkout state; voice stays disconnected until explicitly resumed.
