title: Service and ordering
description: Use voice or touch, hand questions to staff, and continue after stopping voice.

## Choose with help

Use the tabletop iPad to ask about portions, how to eat a dish or drink pairings. You can also search the menu and open product details on screen. Review products and search results shared by voice, then choose the required options and quantity.

TableCast uses registered information. Questions about ingredients, allergens or cross-contact may need the kitchen’s confirmation. Do not treat [food information](./menu-information.md) on screen as an assurance of safety when facts are unconfirmed.

## Confirm an order

Adding to the cart does not place an order. Check products, required choices, quantities, surcharges and the total, then explicitly approve. If prices or availability change, review the updated confirmation. A spoken request or failed submission is not a completed order.

## Ask staff to help

Open the staff assistance control to receive guidance or call staff directly. When a voice conversation needs staff assistance, the questions and relevant products can be handed over. If submission fails, check the displayed state and retry.

Staff review pending calls and handover details in the floor view or table details. Mark a call resolved after addressing it. Acceptance of a call does not mean that staff have arrived or that the problem is resolved.

## Stop and resume voice

Stopping voice stops playback, microphone transmission, transcription and response generation. It does not reconnect until you explicitly resume. Cart, order and billing state remain available, and you can continue using touch. If joining members or changing the service recipient stops voice, resume explicitly using the voice control.

The management Agent Plugin prepares menu and service settings. It is not an entry point for placing a guest’s table order or calling staff on their behalf.
